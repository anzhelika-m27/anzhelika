Why we exist

Adolescence is a critical period of brain development, yet the places where young people learn and grow are not always designed around what we know about the brain. We close the gap between brain science and everyday life, and give youth a real role in shaping brain health.

Mission

Educate K-12 youth on brain health and advocate for policies and environments designed around the brain, through data-driven research.

Vision

A brain-healthy future generation that can navigate the modern and future world.

Our four pillars

Research

Youth-centered studies of what affects brain health, learning readiness and brain capital.

Brain-healthy environments

How architecture, sensory design, schedules and school layout support learning.

Brain health education

Helping students understand their brains and what shapes learning and wellbeing.

Youth leadership & global collaboration

Connecting young people with researchers and organizations worldwide.
