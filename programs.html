Programs & research

Four pillars guide everything we build. Here is what each one looks like in practice.

Research

Youth-centered research on factors affecting brain health, learning readiness and brain capital, including district-scale adolescent brain-health work with our partners.

Brain-healthy environments

Working with neuroarchitecture and healthy-school collaborators on how sensory environments, schedules and design support healthy learning.

Brain health education

Workshops like Meet Your Brain!, led by students and guided by brain-health education experts and physicians.

Youth leadership & global collaboration

Links to researchers, institutions and the global brain-capital community.

Our network

The Council is being built alongside researchers, physicians, educators, architects and brain-health leaders who share one goal: translating neuroscience into practical action for young people.
